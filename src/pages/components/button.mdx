<H2 id="inverted">Inverted</H2>

`isInverted` swaps a variant's palette for one meant to sit on an inverted (dark)
background. Only `primary`, `secondary`, `danger`, `success`, and `warning` define
inverted styles — `ghost` and `outline` are unaffected by `isInverted`.

<H3 id="primary-inverted">Primary Inverted</H3>

<Preview code={`<Button variant="primary" isInverted>Primary</Button>`}>
  <div className="rounded bg-black p-4">
    <Button
      variant="primary"
      isInverted
    >
      {'Primary'}
    </Button>
  </div>
</Preview>
